# Dunning Letter Template — Level 2 (Firm Request)

## Trigger Criteria
- Invoice overdue 60+ days
- Level 1 sent at least 15 days prior
- No active dispute on the invoice
- Customer did not respond to Level 1 or partial payment only

## Tone
Firm but professional. Acknowledges prior communication. Mentions potential consequences without being threatening.

## Subject Line
Past Due Notice — Immediate Attention Required — Account {ACCOUNT_NUMBER}

## Template

Dear {CUSTOMER_NAME},

This is a formal follow-up regarding overdue amounts on your account. Despite our previous reminder dated {LEVEL1_DATE}, the following invoices remain unpaid:

| Invoice | Date | Amount Due | Days Overdue |
|---------|------|-----------|--------------|
{INVOICE_TABLE}

**Total Outstanding: {CURRENCY} {TOTAL_AMOUNT}**

We request immediate payment or contact within 5 business days to discuss resolution options. We understand that circumstances may have changed since our last communication, and we are open to discussing payment arrangements if needed.

Please be advised that continued non-payment may result in:
- A review of the credit terms on your account
- Potential adjustments to your credit limit

We value our business relationship and wish to resolve this matter promptly. Please contact us at your earliest convenience to discuss how we can assist.

To make a payment:
- Bank Transfer: {BANK_DETAILS}
- Online Portal: {PORTAL_URL}
- Contact: {CONTACT_EMAIL} or {CONTACT_PHONE}

If you believe any of these invoices are incorrect or subject to dispute, please notify us immediately with supporting documentation so we can investigate.

Sincerely,
{SENDER_NAME}
Collections Department
{COMPANY_NAME}
{CONTACT_EMAIL}
{CONTACT_PHONE}

## Usage Notes
- Send via email with read receipt requested
- Copy the assigned account manager
- Minimum 15 days after Level 1
- Requires collections manager review before sending
- Do NOT send to strategic accounts without AM confirmation
- Log as EBS note with type "Dunning Level 2 Sent"
- Create follow-up JTF task for 5 business days
