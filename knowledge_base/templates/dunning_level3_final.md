# Dunning Letter Template — Level 3 (Final Notice)

## Trigger Criteria
- Invoice overdue 90+ days
- Level 2 sent at least 15 days prior
- No active dispute on the invoice
- No payment plan in place
- Customer has not made meaningful payment or contact

## Approval Required
- Standard accounts: Director approval
- Strategic accounts (revenue >$500K/year): VP approval
- Accounts >$1M annual revenue: CFO notification

## Tone
Formal, consequential, clear about next steps. Professional but unambiguous about actions that will follow.

## Subject Line
FINAL NOTICE — Account {ACCOUNT_NUMBER} — Immediate Action Required

## Template

Dear {CUSTOMER_NAME},

**FINAL NOTICE**

Despite our previous communications dated {LEVEL1_DATE} and {LEVEL2_DATE}, your account remains significantly past due. We have not received payment or a satisfactory response regarding the following outstanding amounts:

| Invoice | Original Date | Amount Due | Days Overdue |
|---------|--------------|-----------|--------------|
{INVOICE_TABLE}

**Total Past Due: {CURRENCY} {TOTAL_AMOUNT}**
**Oldest Invoice: {MAX_DAYS_OVERDUE} days overdue**

This is our final notice before we proceed with the following actions:

1. **Credit Hold** — Your account will be placed on credit hold, preventing new orders from being processed
2. **Order Suspension** — All open orders and pending shipments will be placed on hold
3. **Collections Escalation** — Your account will be referred to our formal collections process for further action
4. **Credit Reporting** — Continued non-payment may be reported to commercial credit agencies

**To avoid these measures, you must take one of the following actions within 7 calendar days of this notice:**

- Remit full payment of {CURRENCY} {TOTAL_AMOUNT}
- Contact us immediately to arrange an acceptable payment plan
- Provide documentation of any valid disputes

Payment methods:
- Bank Transfer: {BANK_DETAILS}
- Wire Transfer: {WIRE_DETAILS}
- Contact for payment plan: {CONTACT_PHONE}

After {DEADLINE_DATE}, we will proceed with the actions outlined above without further notice.

We strongly encourage you to contact us before this deadline. We remain willing to work with you on a reasonable resolution.

Regards,
{SENDER_NAME}
{SENDER_TITLE}
Collections Department
{COMPANY_NAME}
{CONTACT_EMAIL}
{CONTACT_PHONE}

Reference: {DUNNING_REFERENCE_NUMBER}

## Usage Notes
- Send via email AND registered/certified mail
- Copy: Account Manager, AR Director
- BCC: Legal department (for amounts >$100K)
- Requires director approval before sending (VP for strategic accounts)
- Minimum 15 days after Level 2
- NEVER send between Dec 20 - Jan 5 (holiday blackout)
- NEVER send to accounts with active payment plans
- NEVER send if valid dispute is pending investigation
- Log as EBS note with type "Dunning Level 3 — Final Notice Sent"
- Create JTF task for Day 8: "Execute credit hold if no response"
- Retain copy in customer file for potential legal proceedings
