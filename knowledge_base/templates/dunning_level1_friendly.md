# Dunning Letter Template — Level 1 (Friendly Reminder)

## Trigger Criteria
- Invoice overdue 30+ days
- First contact for this overdue cycle
- No active dispute on the invoice

## Tone
Friendly, professional, assumes oversight. No threat or mention of consequences.

## Subject Line
Payment Reminder — Invoice {INVOICE_NUMBER}

## Template

Dear {CUSTOMER_NAME},

We hope this message finds you well.

We wanted to bring to your attention that the following invoice remains outstanding:

- Invoice Number: {INVOICE_NUMBER}
- Invoice Date: {INVOICE_DATE}
- Original Amount: {CURRENCY} {AMOUNT_ORIGINAL}
- Amount Due: {CURRENCY} {AMOUNT_DUE}
- Due Date: {DUE_DATE}
- Days Overdue: {DAYS_OVERDUE}

We understand that oversights happen and would appreciate your prompt attention to this matter.

Payment can be made via:
- Bank Transfer: {BANK_DETAILS}
- Check: Payable to {COMPANY_NAME}, reference invoice number above

If payment has already been sent, please disregard this notice. If you have any questions about this invoice or need a copy resent, please don't hesitate to contact our accounts receivable team.

Thank you for your continued partnership.

Best regards,
{SENDER_NAME}
Accounts Receivable
{COMPANY_NAME}
{CONTACT_EMAIL}
{CONTACT_PHONE}

## Usage Notes
- Send via email (primary) or printed letter if no email on file
- Do NOT mention credit holds, order impacts, or escalation
- Do NOT send if invoice is disputed (check dispute status first)
- Personalize the greeting if account manager provides context
- Log as EBS note with type "Dunning Level 1 Sent"
