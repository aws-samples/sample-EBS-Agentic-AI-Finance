# SOP: New Customer First Late Payment

## Applies To
Customers with less than 6 months of payment history who miss their first payment.

## Rationale
New customers may have legitimate delays due to onboarding, PO processing, or internal approval workflows. An aggressive first response risks damaging a developing relationship.

## Procedure

### Step 1: Wait Period (7 calendar days after due date)
- Do NOT contact the customer immediately
- Allow for mail transit, bank processing delays, or internal approval queues
- Monitor: check if payment appears in AR_CASH_RECEIPTS_ALL during this period

### Step 2: Courtesy Call (Day 8-10)
- Make a phone call before sending any written notice
- Tone: Helpful, assumes oversight
- Confirm invoice was received
- Confirm correct PO number and billing address
- Ask if there are any questions or issues with the invoice
- Document call outcome in EBS notes

### Step 3: Written Follow-up (Day 11-14 if no payment after call)
- Send Dunning Level 1 (friendly reminder) via email
- Reference the phone conversation
- Include payment instructions and bank details
- Offer to resend invoice if needed

### Step 4: Credit Hold Decision
- Do NOT place credit hold on first occurrence unless:
  - Overdue amount exceeds $25,000
  - Customer is unresponsive to both call and email
  - Customer has been flagged by credit check service
- If amount < $25,000 and customer responded: continue monitoring

### Step 5: Follow-up Task
- Create a JTF follow-up task for 14 calendar days from first contact
- Assignee: Original collections agent
- Priority: Medium
- Note: "New customer first late payment — follow up on payment status"

### Step 6: Escalation (if still unpaid at Day 30)
- Escalate to standard collections process (Dunning Level 1 already sent)
- Reclassify from NORMAL to MEDIUM_RISK
- Brief account manager on situation
- Standard policies now apply going forward

## Key Rules
- NEVER place automatic credit hold on new customer first late payment under $25,000
- ALWAYS attempt phone contact before written dunning
- ALWAYS document all interactions in EBS notes
- Grace period does NOT apply if customer has been flagged by credit bureau
